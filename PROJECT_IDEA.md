# Project Description & Research Question

My project looks at what happens after a stock takes a dip—specifically, how likely it is to rebound and how long that recovery usually takes. I’m pulling ten years of daily Yahoo Finance data (from January 2016 through January 2026) across the S&P 500, S&P MidCap 400, and NASDAQ-100 to see if a company’s size or market capitalization changes its resilience. This is a big deal in trading because 'buying the dip' only works if you can tell a temporary price drop from a permanent decline, giving traders a clear edge on timing and risk management.
