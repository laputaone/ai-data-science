This dataset is compiled directly from Yahoo Finance and tracks daily market activity for roughly 1,000 stocks listed across the S&P 500, S&P MidCap 400, and NASDAQ-100 over a 10-year span from January 2016 to January 2026, totaling roughly 2.5 million rows. The dataset consists of six key columns: t for the trading date, o for opening price, h for daily highest price, l for daily lowest price, c for closing price (adjusted for splits and dividends), and volume (v) to measure how actively the stock was traded that day. A few potential data issues to manage include missing historical data for younger companies that went public after 2016, duplicate records caused by companies belonging to both the S&P 500 and NASDAQ-100, and artificial price drops caused by unadjusted stock splits—all of which require basic data cleaning and deduplication before running our recovery calculations.
